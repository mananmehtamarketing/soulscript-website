I am Marine, founder of SoulScript.

Explorer of the stories we carry. Guide to what else might be possible.

I have always been fascinated by stories and their power to change how we see the world. For 17 years I told other people's stories, for companies and for brands.

Then one day my own body stopped me. Burn-out.
Suddenly the story I had been telling myself no longer held.

So I looked differently at my choices, my beliefs, my ambitions. I listened to the stories that had been guiding my life, often without my knowing. And above all, I asked myself whether I could write what came next differently.

For ten years I searched, explored and took things apart. I learned to listen to my body and my sensations, and to rewrite my own story. SoulScript was born from that path.

Today I work with people who feel that part of their story no longer looks like them, so they can write what comes next differently and, above all, live it.
