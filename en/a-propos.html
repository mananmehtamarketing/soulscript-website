The story of SoulScript

Chapter 01

Why me? Why SoulScript?

Stories have fascinated me for as long as I can remember.
Studying them. Telling them. Transforming them.

I studied literature to explore why certain stories move us, change us, and make us want to believe in something larger than ourselves.

I then began my career in advertising, and for 17 years I put that love of storytelling to work for companies and brands who use the power of their story to create feeling, to win people over and, sometimes, to change the world.

At Airbnb, I told the story of belonging with Belong Anywhere.

With Le Collectionist, I helped turn holidays into extraordinary memories.

At Agence Fantastic, I argued that the stories that stay with us are born of deep conviction.

At Alan, I made prevention a new way of caring for your health.

In the end, I spent years telling other people's stories without realising that the most powerful story was the one I was telling myself.

The one that shaped my choices.
The one that set my limits.
The one that decided, often without my knowing, what life I thought was possible for me.

And that is where everything began to change.

Chapter 02

The day the story went off the rails

At 28, with the job I had dreamed of in a London advertising agency, everything stopped.

One morning, the alarm goes off.
I try to get up.
My legs are paralysed.
They simply refuse to carry me.

My body had (finally) rebelled.
I was exhausted, burnt out from the inside.

That burn-out was not the end of my story. It was the beginning of its greatest rewrite.

Chapter 03

Ten years to rewrite my story

Healing did not mean becoming who I had been. It meant becoming who I had never dared to be. For ten years I explored what could help me build a life that was truer, freer and more deeply aligned.

I refused to let the ghosts of my past keep holding the pen. Because as long as they stayed in the shadows, my stories were writing the reality I was living in.

I went back through the wounds, the family dramas, the sense of abandonment, the trauma of burn-out, the fear of never being enough and that relentless need to prove my worth.

Sentence after sentence.
Belief after belief.
Choice after choice.

Until I had written a story I could finally breathe in.

The SoulScript method
Chapter 04

The birth of SoulScript

Today I know something I wish I had understood sooner: you can change your job, your city, your partner, your life or your therapist. But if you keep telling yourself the same story, you will repeat the same patterns.

Change does not begin with what we do. It begins with the story we are acting from.

I looked for answers in the body, in breathwork, in movement therapy, in Ayurveda, in neuroscience, in Kundalini yoga.

I brought those disciplines together to create SoulScript: a space to understand what holds us back, loosen what keeps us stuck, and find the freedom to decide our own story.

Today I help people who feel trapped in an old script to pick up the pen again, find their voice, and write what comes next differently.

Because deep down, we do not always need to change our life. Sometimes we simply need to change the story we are living it from.
